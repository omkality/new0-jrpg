Crypt furnishings are original modular meshes: stone sarcophagi, urn shelves,
memorial altars, supply chests and a barrel. They occupy floor space and share
explicit collision footprints with the world layout. The former freestanding
fenced arches have been removed.

Forest trunk bases are grounded to the exact rendered triangulated terrain.
Bark, leaves, understory, candles, iron and other meshes use scene fog.
Shrubs share the existing instanced foliage draw call. All candle flames share
a single instanced mesh with GPU deformation and no per-frame matrix upload.
No additional dynamic light pool has been introduced.
